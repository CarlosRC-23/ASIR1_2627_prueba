Conclusion & Our Opinion

In our opinion, social networks offer incredible tools to stay connected and informed, but the price we pay in personal data is often too high and poorly explained to everyday users.

We believe digital education and stricter data protection laws are essential. Users should not have to give up their privacy just to participate in modern online communication.
